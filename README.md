### **Hi there** 👋

Welcome to my GitHub!

Here you'll find my portfolio of projects using Power BI to visualise key business information.

I have a background in software testing, data analysis and marketing. Marketing was where I was first exposed to data analysis by tracking performance with excel spreadsheets and creating reports for management, thus sparking my interest in analysing data and using it to drive decisions. As a creative person, being able to translate what the data is telling us through pleasing visuals has become my passion.

Now, in my current role, I get to combine testing with data analysis and visualisation. I build and optimise dashboards that uncover hidden insights, validate data accuracy, and help stakeholders and clients understand what’s really going on.

### **Projects**
---
A brief rundown of the projects I have worked on outside of work:

> **Freshli EoY Trend Analysis**

**Description:** This project was created to clean data with a dataset from Kaggle for a retail store. Beyond cleaning the data I also provided end of year marketing insights so that the marketing team can present the findings to stakeholders as well as identify potential areas of improvement for the year ahead.

**Reports and conclusions:** [`Freshli EoY Analysis`](https://github.com/natacha-dasilva/Freshli-EoY-Analysis)

> **FBI Crime Analysis**

**Description:** This project was created to analyse a dataset collected from the FBI (which was combined and uploaded to Kaggle). The dashboard was created to continuously identify patterns throughout the current year among victims by gender, descent and age as well as reocurring crimes in certain locations or during certain American holidays and special days.

**Reports and conclusions:** [`FBI Crime Statistics`](https://github.com/natacha-dasilva/FBI-Crime-Stats)  
  
> **Office of Rail and Road Payments Analysis**

**Description:** This project was created to combine monthly data from the Office of Rail and Road, specifically for the data that relates to payments above £25,000 which has to be disclosed to the public. The dashboard created allows staff to access quick visuals for sharing trends and figures online, or in the news, making it easier for the public to see and understand what the payments are and for what purpose. It also allows ORR teams to analyse where their biggest spends are, and how long it takes to pay after the transaction has been allocated.

**Reports and conclusions:** [`ORR Every Day Dashboard`](https://github.com/natacha-dasilva/ORR-Every-Day-Dashboard)
